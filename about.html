About Us

Welcome to Digital Tools Hub, India's leading 100% free web utility store designed specifically for small business owners, retail shopkeepers, local traders, farmers, and daily wage workers.

"Our mission is to replace old paper logbooks with simple, high-speed, and secure digital utility interfaces that require absolutely zero login or setup charges."

Who We Are

Digital Tools Hub was founded by Raju Jatapara, a passionate designer and developer committed to making accounting and utility templates accessible to everyone. We noticed that small business owners, especially in rural and semi-urban sectors, find modern ERP and accounting software too expensive, complex, or slow for daily client billing. We built this platform to simplify their calculations.

What We Provide

We host a suite of 25+ powerful tools including:

  • GST Invoicing & Cash Memos: Issue quotes and receipts directly on mobile.
  • Agricultural & Services Tools: Perform APMC crop bag calculations, mill fat charts, and tractor rent sheets.
  • Business Marketing Banners: Design visiting cards, festival posters, letterheads, and wedding invitations.

Why Choose Digital Tools Hub?

We are a privacy-first platform. All of our utility calculators work directly inside your browser. No registration is required, no phone number checks are executed, and all tools remain 100% free forever.
