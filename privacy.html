Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Digital Tools Hub, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Digital Tools Hub and how we use it.

1. Local Processing & Zero Server Storage

Our tools are designed as client-side applications. Any details you type into the invoices, calculators, visiting cards, resume builders, or other utility services are processed entirely inside your local web browser. We do NOT transmit, save, or store your private inputs, business names, customer lists, financial data, or generated documents on any external servers.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Like any other website, Digital Tools Hub uses standard technologies to improve the user experience:

  • Local Storage: We use browser localStorage to remember your theme preferences (Dark or Light mode) and your language selections (English, Gujarati, or Hindi).
  • Third-Party Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Policy

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
